Four things we could build for Retool

Each one runs in a Clay table that your ops team can change without help from engineering. Clay syncs the results to your CRM and sequencer.

Orchestration

Route every signup to the right rep.

When someone creates a Retool workspace, Clay looks up their company, headcount and tech stack. If the company fits your enterprise profile, Clay sends the workspace to an AE.

  • Match signups from personal email addresses to a company and role
  • Group workspaces that belong to the same parent company
  • Score each account on product usage and company fit
See it built in the demo
Data

Check 200+ data providers instead of one.

Clay asks providers one at a time until one returns the email, phone number or company data you need. In large global accounts one provider usually leaves gaps, so this raises coverage.

Anthropic tripled their enrichment rate with Clay's data marketplace.

  • Find the full buying committee in every region
  • Get verified work emails and direct dials
  • Pay only when a provider returns a result

Agents

Research 1,500 accounts a week with the reps you have.

Claygent reads job posts, engineering blogs and annual reports for signs that matter to Retool, such as a company hiring internal tools engineers or starting an AI ops program. Before a rep opens the account, Claygent has written a short brief and a suggested first line.

Should you build this in-house?

Your team could build an enrichment pipeline. The harder part is the work around it. You'd need contracts with 200+ data providers, logic for which one to call first, deduplication, and someone to keep it running. Clay handles that, and anything you build in Retool can sit on top of it.
